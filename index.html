How the checks work

In the tables: formal = statement formally verified · recomputed = answer recomputed · numeric = numerically checked · model-reviewed = reviewed by a model only.

A passing Lean proof establishes the conclusion; it does not certify each step of the written solution in the PDF.

LabelWhat was checkedWhat was not checked
Statement formally verifiedThe conclusion of the subquestion has a proof accepted by Lean.Each step of the written solution in the PDF.
Answer recomputedAn independent script recomputed the final value or expression from the question's conditions and obtained the same answer.The written explanation of intermediate steps.
Numerically checkedThe equality held in random numerical examples.The general case.
Reviewed onlyModel review only.—
